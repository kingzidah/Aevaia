Why they are here
-----------------
This piece began as a private birthday gift built for one person. It is now
published as a portfolio demo, and the owner asked that her own photographs not
be published with it. These stand in for them so the interactions — the photo
stack that fans open, the modal, the gallery — can be shown without publishing
anybody's private pictures.
